Research Interests

My research develops statistical and machine-learning methods for large-scale, multimodal functional and spatio-temporal data. I am interested in representing structured and heterogeneous signals, interpreting predictions under dependent predictors, and quantifying uncertainty for temporally dependent and rare-event data. My current research includes:

  • Rare-event detection and real-time forecasting in space weather, including solar energetic particle events, proton flux, and multivariate geomagnetic indices.
  • Structured learning for multimodal spatio-temporal data using functional representations, multivariate time-series models, and machine learning.
  • Interpretable machine learning and uncertainty quantification under temporal dependence and correlated predictors.
  • Irregular functional data analysis with latent states and heterogeneous covariance structures, motivated by wearable sensing and gait data.
  • Statistical methods for biomedical research, including adaptive clinical-trial design and psychometric scale development and validation.

Research Overview: My doctoral research focused on statistical modeling of irregular functional data, including shape-constrained trajectories and functional observations with heterogeneous covariance structures. Motivated by wearable sensor data from individuals with neurodegenerative diseases, I developed Bayesian, latent-factor, and continuous-time hidden Markov models for trajectory recovery, classification, and anomaly detection. I also worked with Prof. Siu Hung Cheung on adaptive clinical-trial designs for ordinal outcomes under sequential monitoring and response-adaptive randomization, and collaborated with clinicians at Shenzhen People's Hospital to develop and statistically validate the Neuro-11 questionnaire for screening somatic symptom disorder.

My current postdoctoral research extends these interests to space-weather forecasting. I develop deep-learning and multivariate functional time-series methods for forecasting rare solar energetic particle events, proton flux, and geomagnetic activity from multiple sources of solar and solar-wind observations. This work has led me to broader statistical questions about feature importance analysis, interpretable prediction, calibrated uncertainty, and reliable learning under temporal dependence.
